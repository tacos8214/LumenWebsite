The story behind Lumen

A little more calm.
A little more wonder.

Lumen is an ambient TV experience built around the beauty of the natural world.

Our vision

Technology that lets you slow down.

Screens do not always need to demand our attention. Sometimes, they can offer a moment of peace: the movement of the ocean, the quiet of a forest, or the beauty of a mountain landscape.


Lumen is being developed to bring that feeling into your living space through cinematic ambient video experiences designed for Android TV.

Our Purpose

Create a simple, beautiful way to enjoy nature-inspired visuals on the big screen.

Our Approach

Focus on an elegant interface, immersive visuals and a straightforward TV experience.

Our Future

Continue developing Lumen and improving the experience as the project grows.
